Heterogeneous devices and software configurations can shift scientific training time toward installation troubleshooting. This paper describes a reproducible workspace architecture based on Git repositories, development containers, notebooks, and automation, contextualized in SILAFAE XV and the 2026 CERN School of Computing in Latin America. The approach connects environment preparation, analysis execution, and preservation of results. It reports the author's observation of preparation for 80 students in less than ten minutes, while specifying that interpretation requires an operational definition of the interval and supporting records. This observation is distinguished from a controlled assessment of learning. As a complementary demonstrator, the manuscripts themselves are maintained in LaTeX and compiled to PDF through continuous integration. Connectivity, quotas, maintenance, portability, and a reproducible evaluation protocol for future schools are discussed.
