Title: Open-licensed hackathons for community building and outreach in Latin America

LAA-HECAP was established following regional strategic-planning efforts and has grown into a large community (600+ members in 2025), providing a natural platform to scale education and outreach actions across Latin America. We present the CoAfina hackathon as a concrete, reproducible outreach model that translates open-science principles into hands-on capacity building. Co-organized within the LA-CoNGA ecosystem by Creative Commons (CC) Venezuela, RedCLARA, and CEDIA, CoAfina is designed around team-based projects that reuse openly accessible datasets and open-source tools while explicitly embedding open licensing from the start. Reports document growth from 50 students (2022) to 78 students (2023) all the way to 90 students in 2025, spanning 50 universities across 15 countries. A key differentiator is systematic publication of all training materials under CC0 licenses (github.com/laa-hecap/CoAfina-resources), enabling reuse and lowering barriers for new cohorts. CC Venezuela brings proven expertise in organizing virtual collaborative events, including ICTP-PWF honored School of Physics, or bilingual "Health for All" editathon (2021, with Wikipedia articles in Spanish and English under WHO guidelines, funding and personnel). We discuss operational choices improving inclusiveness under heterogeneous connectivity and hardware conditions (distributed collaboration, lightweight onboarding, mentorship), and outline how networks connect hackathon participants to longer-term training pathways.
